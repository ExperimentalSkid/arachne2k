# Analyst workflows

## Start with the work queues

The overview explains what is new, what is urgent, what remains unclassified,
and whether collection is healthy. Urgent is evidence-based and intentionally
narrow; **Needs classification** is uncertainty, not an allegation.

Use:

- **Spain Priority** for destination-specific Spain evidence and direct source
  links.
- **Pages to check** for bounded analyst decisions.
- **Site Cards / Directory** for stable host-level identity and representative
  pages.
- **Connections, public aliases, and identity signals** for evidence-backed
  relationships, not generic handle matches.
- **Watchlist and timeline** for saved scope and change history.
- **Quality Center** for contradictions, weak classification, and repairable
  consistency states.

## Review a Site Card

1. Confirm the displayed source host and representative page.
2. Separate site type, page type, content category, safety state, risk, analyst
   importance, Spain relevance, and urgency.
3. Inspect supporting and contradicting evidence before changing a category.
4. Use an analyst title for a cleaner label; it does not change source evidence,
   risk, category, or Spain state.
5. Add notes or a report only when the card supports a durable review outcome.
6. Use re-check or bounded enrichment for missing public identity evidence; do
   not use it to overwrite analyst decisions.

## Spain review

A Spanish-language path or text alone is not Spain Priority. A priority item
must show destination-specific evidence such as country, location, target,
shipping, seller, actor, or financial context. Open the exact evidence page,
record the analyst decision, and clear the review state when complete.

## Identity review

One profile can hold multiple handles, roles, identifiers, evidence records,
relationships, and timeline events. A handle-only similarity is a candidate,
not an identity. Exact identifiers, independent evidence families, and
contradictions must remain visible. Analyst rejection and confirmation survive
recalculation.

## Restricted material

Restricted child destinations do not classify their parent. Policy-hidden
metadata may be visible only in the controlled advanced view. Do not attempt to
force a preview, screenshot, snippet, or expansion for a blocked destination.
Use the correction control only for a confirmed false positive.

## Reports

Reports use the analyst title when present and retain detected title/source
metadata for traceability. A report is a review artifact, not proof that the
source is true or current.
